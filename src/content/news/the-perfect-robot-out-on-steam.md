[The Perfect Robot](/games/the-perfect-robot/) is out now on [Steam](https://store.steampowered.com/app/4367790/The_Perfect_Robot/), with 25% off for the first two weeks.

## Your robot does exactly what you tell it. That's the problem.

Lay down four move cards, press EXECUTE, and watch your robot carry them out to the letter across conveyor belts, spinning gears, ice and lasers. One wrong card and it rolls straight off the edge. Get every card right and it's perfect.

## Six worlds, 57 hand-made levels

- **Tutorial**: one new idea per level.
- **Workshop**: solo puzzles with a fixed hand, and crates to push.
- **Clockwork**: turntables, and a lever that turns every machine round.
- **Frost**: ice that slides you until something stops you, and thin ice that holds only once.
- **Sky Rally**: race rival robots across floating islands.
- **Halloween**: a haunted island where ghosts scare your robot round and crypts carry it across the board.

## Play together

Race or battle friends online, or take on robot rivals in Quick Game. Steam achievements and Steam Cloud saves, and you can play with a controller.

Thank you for playing!
