[The Perfect Robot](/games/the-perfect-robot/) comes out on Steam on **22 October 2026 at 18:00 CEST**. You can [wishlist it on Steam](https://store.steampowered.com/app/4367790/The_Perfect_Robot/) now, and Steam will tell you the moment it is out.

## What it is

Your robot does exactly what you tell it. That's the problem. Lay down four move cards, press EXECUTE, and watch your robot carry them out to the letter across conveyor belts, spinning gears, ice and lasers. One wrong card and it rolls straight off the edge.

The Steam version has everything from the phone version: 57 hand-made levels in six worlds, from the Tutorial to the haunted Halloween island, Quick Game against bots and online races and battles with friends.

## Made for a PC

- Play with mouse and keyboard (number keys place cards, Enter runs the program) or with a controller.
- Steam achievements, one for every world you finish and one for every world you earn all the stars in.
- Steam Cloud keeps your progress between computers.
- Resolution, window mode and interface size settings, with layouts that fit a Steam Deck screen.

There is a launch discount of 25% for the first two weeks.
